Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

WEGO Parking ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data when you use the WEGO app.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: Your email address, collected when you create an account or sign in with Google. If you use Google Sign-In, we also receive your name from Google.
  • Location data: Your device's current location, used to broadcast your parking spot to nearby drivers, privately save where you parked, and show available spots near you. Location is only accessed when you use the app.
  • Profile photo: An optional photo you upload to personalize your profile.
  • Usage data: The number of parking spots you have shared and found, broadcast history used to calculate community contributions, and optional coarse private parking signals used to improve HeatMap AI.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To display your parking spot broadcast to nearby drivers in real time.
  • To show you available parking spots on the map.
  • To send you parking alerts for your saved zones.
  • To warn you about upcoming street cleaning at your logged parking location.
  • To privately remember your parked car location for your account only.
  • To improve HeatMap AI using coarse, non-public parked-car signals when this setting is enabled.
  • To track your community contributions toward earned Premium status.
  • To display your profile photo and email within the app.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only in the following ways:

  • Other WEGO users: When you broadcast your spot, your vehicle type and approximate location are visible to nearby drivers. Your privately saved parked-car location is not shown publicly. Your email and profile photo are never shared publicly.
  • WEGO HeatMap AI: If Private HeatMap Signals are enabled, WEGO may use a coarse grid location, day of week, and time bucket from your saved parked-car event. This signal does not include your exact parked-car coordinates or public identity.
  • Google Firebase: We use Firebase for authentication, database storage, and file storage. Data is processed in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • NYC Open Data: We query public street cleaning data to provide alerts. No personal information is sent to this service.

4. Data Retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account is active. Broadcast history is retained for up to 12 months to calculate community contributions. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data.
  • Withdraw consent for location access at any time through your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

WEGO is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including Firebase's built-in security rules and encrypted data transmission, to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
